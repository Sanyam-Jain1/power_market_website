The 1pm / 10pm rule

[Author] · [Date] · [X] min read

Charge at one in the afternoon, when the sun has pushed prices down. Sell at ten at night, when it has gone and the country turns its lights on. It is the simplest schedule a battery can run.

How much of a battery's possible earnings does that one rule capture? We test it against the best schedule in hindsight, on every day in the record, and look at the days it gets wrong.
